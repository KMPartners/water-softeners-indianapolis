Tested to the standard

Commercial standby systems are maintained and tested under NFPA 110, the standard for emergency and standby power, including periodic exercise and load-bank testing where required.

Commercial standby questions

What size does my business need?

It depends on what must stay running : refrigeration, HVAC, POS, lighting, life-safety. We focus on natural-gas standby in the 30–60 kW band that fits most small businesses, confirmed with a load calc.

Do you handle large diesel / critical systems?

Our sweet spot is light-commercial natural gas. For large diesel above ~60 kW, paralleling, or NFPA 110 Level 1 critical work, we'll point you to the right specialist rather than overreach.

What is NFPA 110 load-bank testing?

NFPA 110 is the standard for standby power systems. Units need periodic exercise and, where required, load-bank testing proving the generator carries its rated load. We provide that testing and documentation.

Will it keep my cooler and POS up?

Yes . That's the point. A single outage can spoil a cooler and stop sales. We size standby power to keep refrigeration, lighting, and point-of-sale running so a storm doesn't close you.
