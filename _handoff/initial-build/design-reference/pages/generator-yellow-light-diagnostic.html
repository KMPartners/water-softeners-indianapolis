A green light means ready. A yellow light means “not quite”, and a generator that isn't quite ready is the one that lets you down at 2 a.m. during a storm.

Yellow most often signals a maintenance reminder or a stored fault code : a tired battery, a missed service interval, an exercise that didn't complete. We read the Evolution controller to see exactly what it logged, fix the underlying cause rather than just clearing the light, and confirm the unit starts and transfers under load. As a factory-authorized dealer, that work stays warranty-compliant.

Yellow-light & fault-code questions

My installer is out of business, can you help?

Yes . Adopting orphaned Generac units is exactly what take-over service is for. We diagnose it, bring it back to ready, set up monitoring, and put you on a plan, all as a factory-authorized dealer.

Can you do warranty work you didn't install?

Yes, and only a Generac authorized service dealer can perform warranty-compliant repairs. Using an uncertified tech can risk your coverage, so transferring service to us protects it.

I bought a house with one, does it work?

We find out: inspect it, run it under load to confirm it transfers and powers the home, read the controller, set up Mobile Link, and tell you honestly what shape it's in.

What does the yellow light mean?

Usually a maintenance reminder or a stored fault code on the Evolution controller. We read the code, fix the cause, and return the unit to steady green-ready.

Do you take over other brands?

We service Kohler, Briggs, Honeywell, and Champion as brand-agnostic take-over work. We're not an authorized warranty dealer for those, so it's standard service rather than warranty-compliant repair.
