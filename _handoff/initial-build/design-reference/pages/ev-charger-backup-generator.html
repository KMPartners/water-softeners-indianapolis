EV-charger backup questions

Can it run my EV charger in an outage?

Yes. A properly sized standby generator powers your Level 2 charger along with the home, automatically, within seconds of an outage.

Does an EV change the size I need?

Yes . Level 2 charging is a large continuous load. We size the generator, often with a Smart Management Module, so the charger and the home are covered without overloading the unit.

Charge and run the house at once?

With the right sizing, yes. Where demand is high, load management sheds non-essential circuits during peak so your charger and essentials stay powered.

Whole-house or just the charger?

Either works. We size to what you want protected (the charger and critical circuits, or the entire home) and show you both paths.
