Ready means tested, not just installed

Standby generators sit idle for months, then are asked to carry a whole home on a moment's notice. Battery, fuel, oil, and the controller all drift over time. A maintenance rhythm, and remote monitoring, is what turns "it was working last year" into "it started tonight." In Fishers and Noblesville that matters most during Duke Energy and AES Indiana storm season; in Anderson, Anderson Municipal Light & Power.

Why warranty-compliant matters

Only a Generac authorized service dealer can perform warranty-compliant repairs. Letting an uncertified tech work on the unit can put your remaining coverage at risk, so moving service to us protects the warranty you already paid for.

Service & repair questions

Do you service generators you didn't install?

Yes . Take-over service is a core part of what we do. We adopt orphaned Generac units, inspect generators that came with a home, and pick up where a vanished installer left off, warranty-compliant as a factory-authorized dealer.

What does a maintenance plan include?

Scheduled service of what keeps a unit ready (oil and filter, battery, plugs, and a load test) plus remote monitoring. Plans cover both our installs and take-over units from any installer.

My Generac has a yellow light, is that urgent?

Usually a maintenance reminder or stored fault code, not an emergency, but it does mean the unit may not be fully ready for the next outage. We read the code, fix the cause, and return it to green-ready.

Should I repair or replace an older unit?

It depends on age, hours, and what's failing. We give an honest repair-vs-replace evaluation with the numbers both ways, so you're not pouring money into a unit near the end of its life.
