What the switch does

It's the brain of the system: without an automatic transfer switch, a generator is just a box in the yard.

✓Switches to generator power in seconds
✓Isolates the home from the grid (line-worker safe)
✓Sized and matched to your panel

A generator makes the power; the transfer switch decides, safely and automatically, when and how your home uses it.

When the utility drops, the automatic transfer switch disconnects your home from the grid, signals the generator to start, and moves the house onto generator power within seconds, then reverses it all when utility returns. That grid isolation is what keeps power from back-feeding the lines and protects the Duke Energy and AES Indiana crews working the outage. We size the switch to your panel, pull the permit, and have it inspected.

Whole-home or managed-load?

There's more than one way to switch a home. We run an NEC 220 load calc and recommend the switch that fits your panel and budget, no oversizing.

Transfer switch questions

What does an ATS do?

It makes backup automatic : it senses the outage, disconnects the home from the grid, starts the generator, and switches the house over within seconds, then back when utility returns.

Whole-home or managed-load?

A whole-home switch backs the entire panel; a managed-load switch sheds big loads at peak so a right-sized generator covers the house. We recommend from your load calc.

Why must a pro install it?

It ties into your service entrance and must isolate the home before energizing, critical for safety and line workers. It needs a licensed electrician, permit, and inspection.

Will I need a panel upgrade?

Sometimes . Older or full panels may need a service upgrade or sub-panel. We confirm with an NEC 220 load calc before quoting.
