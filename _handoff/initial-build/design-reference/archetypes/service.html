01
Engineering
& why

Sized to your home, never beyond it

Generator sizing isn't about the square footage of your house . It's about the electrical load you actually want to keep running. Two 3,000 sq ft homes can need very different units depending on whether they heat with gas or electric, run one air conditioner or two, and have a well pump, EV charger, or finished basement in the mix. That's why we start every job with an NEC 220 load calculation rather than a rule of thumb or a guess off a sales sheet.

For the large majority of Fishers and Noblesville homes, 22 kW is the unit that calc points to: enough to run the whole house at once on natural gas, without paying for liquid-cooled capacity you'll never draw. If your load runs higher (electric heat, dual HVAC, heavy EV charging) we'll show you the math for stepping up to 24–26 kW instead of overselling you a unit “just in case.”

And where a panel sits right at the line, a Smart Management Module sheds non-essential circuits (think: the second A/C, the oven) for the brief moments of peak demand, so you stay whole-house-capable on a right-sized generator instead of jumping to the next price tier.

04
Local
notes

Built for Fishers storms & Fishers rules

Across Hamilton County, the power you're backing up is usually Duke Energy or AES Indiana; in the city of Anderson it's Anderson Municipal Light & Power. The failure modes here are wind and ice in the cold months and derecho-style thunderstorm clusters in summer, the kind of multi-day outages that emptied freezers and shut down sump pumps across the county in the April 2025 storms.

Placement matters as much as sizing. We site the unit to manufacturer and code setbacks from doors, windows, and the meter, and we keep it HOA-conscious, tucked to the side yard and screened where covenants ask for it, because a lot of Fishers neighborhoods have rules about it.

Before any gas-line work we file an Indiana 811 locate, and we coordinate any gas-meter upsizing with the utility so the generator never runs starved for fuel.

05
FAQ
Will a 22 kW really run my whole house?

For most Fishers and Noblesville homes on natural gas, yes : A/C, furnace blower, sump or well pump, kitchen, and living circuits, all at once. The load calc confirms it for your specific home, and where demand runs high a Smart Management Module keeps you whole-house-capable without jumping to a bigger unit.

How long does the installation take?

Once the permit is pulled and the gas work is scheduled, most 22 kW installs are completed and commissioned within one to two days. We'll give you the timeline for your home with the quote.

Natural gas or propane, which should I choose?

If your home already has natural gas, as most developed Fishers and Noblesville neighborhoods do, that's almost always the answer: it's an essentially unlimited supply, so the generator runs as long as the outage lasts. Propane is the move for rural properties without a gas main; we'll size the tank so an extended outage doesn't run you dry.

How loud is it, and how often does it run?

Day to day, it's quiet : the unit runs a brief weekly self-test (its “exercise” cycle) and is otherwise silent until the power actually goes out. The enclosure is built for low-dB operation; we'll confirm the rating for your model and place it to keep sound away from bedrooms and patios.

What does it cost, and why fixed-bid?

Total cost depends on the unit, your gas and electrical setup, and the placement run, which is exactly why we quote a transparent, fixed bid after the on-site load calc instead of a vague “starting at” number. You'll know the price before we start, and it won't move on you mid-job.

Who services it after the install?

We do. As a factory-authorized dealer we perform warranty-compliant maintenance and repairs ourselves . You're not handed off to a call center or left chasing the original installer. Only an authorized service dealer can do warranty work, so this keeps your coverage intact.
