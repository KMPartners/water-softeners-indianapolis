Sizing & install questions

What size standby generator do I need?

It's set by the load you want to keep running, not square footage. Most Fishers and Noblesville homes run the whole house on 22 kW; partial-home setups use 14–18 kW, and large homes with electric heat or EV charging may need 24–26 kW. The on-site NEC 220 load calc gives the exact answer.

Air-cooled or liquid-cooled?

Air-cooled units (10–26 kW) cover the vast majority of homes and cost far less to buy and install. Liquid-cooled units handle much larger loads for estates and light-commercial buildings. Most Fishers homes are well served by an air-cooled Guardian.

Natural gas or propane?

If your home has natural gas, that's almost always best: an essentially unlimited supply, so the unit runs as long as the outage lasts. Propane suits rural properties without a gas main, and we size the tank for extended outages.

Do I need a permit?

Yes. A standby install needs an electrical permit, and gas and placement must meet code. We pull the permit with your local authority, file an Indiana 811 locate before digging, and handle the inspection.
