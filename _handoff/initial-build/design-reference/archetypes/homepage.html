Common questions

What size whole-house generator do I need?

It depends on the electrical load you want to keep running, not square footage. Most Fishers and Noblesville homes run the whole house on a 22 kW unit; larger homes with electric heat or EV charging may need 24–26 kW. We confirm it with an on-site NEC 220 load calculation.

How much does it cost to install?

Cost depends on the unit, your gas and electrical setup, and the placement run, so we give a transparent fixed-bid quote after an on-site load calc, not a vague "starting at." You'll know the price before we begin.

Do you service generators you didn't install?

Yes. We take over orphaned Generac units, inspect generators that came with a home you just bought, and diagnose yellow-light fault codes, all warranty-compliant as a factory-authorized dealer.

How long does installation take?

Once the permit is pulled and gas work is scheduled, most home standby installs are completed and commissioned within one to two days.

Are you an authorized Generac dealer?

Yes . Meridian is a factory-authorized Generac dealer, so our installs and repairs are warranty-compliant. Only an authorized service dealer can perform warranty work without putting your coverage at risk.

Every install is wired as an optional standby system under the National Electrical Code, to spec and to permit.
