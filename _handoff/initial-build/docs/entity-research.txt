Key Findings
1. Market & outage context (urgency entity drivers)
Fishers is a high-income, high-turnover, fast-growing suburb. Per World Population
Review, "Fishers has a 2026 population of 105,932… currently growing at a rate of 1.07%
annually… median household income in Fishers is $130,203." Median home value sits
around $405,882 (U.S. News/ACS; Data USA lists a 2024 median property value of $391,000),
and Redfin/Movoto data show hundreds of homes trading monthly (525 sold in Fishers in
September 2025 alone). This combination — affluent + high resale velocity + ongoing new
construction — is ideal for both new Generac installs AND the "bought a house with a
Generac" inheritance funnel.
Outages are frequent and storm-driven. Per a Duke Energy news release (Plainfield, Ind.,
April 3, 2025), early-April 2025 storms ripped through Indiana "causing more than 100,000
power outages across nearly all of the 28 districts the company serves," with "more than 500
additional crew members" brought in for hardest-hit counties including Hamilton; AES

2. The Generac dealer-tier trust signal (positioning lever)
Per Generac's official help center, dealer designations (PowerPro, Direct, Select, Elite, Premiere,
Prestige) "mainly signify whether the dealer is authorized for sales, installation, and/or
maintenance. Higher tiers can additionally perform service and warranty work. PowerPro is an
additional combining designation that means they offer financing options." Key consumer-facing
facts to weaponize in content:
Only a Generac Independent Authorized Service Dealer (IASD) can perform warranty
repairs — "Hiring an uncertified technician puts your coverage at risk." This is the single
most important trust claim available to a brand-new IASD, and it applies fully at launch.
PowerPro Premier historically required multiple factory-trained technicians and a ~92%
customer-satisfaction threshold; PowerPro Elite adds 24/7 support and larger parts
inventory. The honest framing while at IASD: "factory-authorized, warranty-compliant,
and we answer the phone" — trust signals that don't require the higher badge.
3. Cross-shopping: Generac vs Kohler (content entities)
The dominant cross-shop question is "Is Kohler worth the extra money over Generac?"
Consistent finding across sources: Generac = accessible volume leader / "good value for money" /
lower upfront cost / widest installer + parts network; Kohler = premium, heavier build, often cross-
shopped on the 20RESA vs Generac 22kW Guardian. A real installer on Electrician Talk
(hundreds of air- and liquid-cooled units, 600+ service agreements) summarized the modern
consensus: "Generac is a completely different company today. It's a much better product… there
are more competent Generac dealers available to choose from for service, parts, maintenance and
warranty than any other manufacturer." This validates a Generac-default, Kohler-as-premium-
alternative content stance — and supports Kohler take-over service (not Kohler authorization).

5. THE POSITIONING GAP (the wedge)
Among the Generac-authorized field, the standard pattern is either (a) high-volume Premier
dealers with opaque "call for quote" pricing and franchise scale, or (b) HVAC/electrical generalists
for whom generators are a side line. The transparent, fixed-bid, "we answer the phone and
service what we install" positioning is largely unoccupied — only Midwest Generator Solutions
and Williams Comfort Air gesture at it, and neither is a generator-pure-play pinned in Fishers.
Customer-language fatigue patterns (dealer ghosting post-install, being upsized beyond need,
multi-month warranty waits) map directly onto this gap.
6. THE TIER GAP
Two "Premier" badges (Generator Supercenter, Ashpaugh) and one "Elite" (Burtner) anchor the
map pack; the rest are standard Authorized. A new IASD will NOT out-badge them at launch.
The early-marketing answer is to (a) compete on the IASD warranty-compliance trust claim
(which is binary, not tiered), (b) win on transparent pricing and responsiveness where the
Premier players are weakest, and (c) climb to PowerPro Premier as install volume justifies, then
add the badge to GBP/site/truck wraps. Until then, avoid implying a tier you don't hold.
